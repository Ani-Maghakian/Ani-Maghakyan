Updated: 2026-09-03

## Objective

Make the official Ani Maghakyan site the clearest first-party source for her identity, filmography, key projects, professional services and verified source trail across Armenian, Russian and English search. Every new page gets:

- unique title and meta description;
- canonical URL;
- HY/EN/RU hreflang + x-default;
- visible internal links;

- source/evidence section where applicable;
- link back to the filmography/contact path;
- sitemap inclusion;
- IndexNow submission after deployment.

## Evidence policy

The official archive is a first-party source. External databases are corroborating sources, not absolute masters.

Known discrepancies must be preserved rather than hidden. Examples observed during research:

- `Alien`: the official archive and some IMDb views expose different episode counts.
- `Paper Dream`: official author archive and the Seoul Drama Awards entry use different episode counts.
- `Dear Sahmi`: the author archive and IMDb classify the work differently.

Rule: never silently overwrite first-party production records from a third-party database. State which source says what, and correct only after verification.

## Technical SEO acceptance criteria

- robots.txt: important pages allowed
- sitemap.xml: all important canonical URLs included
- canonical: self-referencing and consistent
- hreflang: reciprocal HY/EN/RU/x-default
- no critical content hidden behind client-only rendering
- one clear H1 per standalone page
- crawlable `<a href>` internal links to every priority page
- structured Person + CreativeWork/TVSeries + ProfilePage data
- image discoverability for the official hero image
- IndexNow after deployment
- no hidden text, cloaking, doorway pages or keyword stuffing

## Content system

Content must follow: **evidence → original first-party insight → useful explanation → next action**.

Do not produce mass generic AI articles. Future pages should be published only when they contain at least one of:

- firsthand production insight;
- original script-development method;
- verified project archive material;
- original visual/reference material the rights holder may publish;
- source-backed project history;
- useful tool/template/checklist based on real production practice.

Priority content cluster after the first release:

1. How Elen's Diary was developed — first-party case study
2. Summer of '84 — creator/showrunner/production case study
3. From TV series to first theatre script: Blockade
4. Mi Gexecik Or — current project development notes when publishable
5. Writers' room workflow at Maghakian Scripts
6. Story architecture / series bible / development workflow

## Three growth scenarios

These are planning scenarios, not guarantees.

### Conservative — 90 days

- 30 new evidence-rich localized URLs indexed or submitted
- brand/entity SERP begins consolidating around the official source
- first 5–10 quality source/referral links
- clear baseline from Search Console

### Base — 6 months

- 20–30 high-value first-party pages/case studies beyond the initial archive
- official site consistently competitive on brand + project-author queries
- 10–20 quality referring domains
- measurable non-branded impressions and professional inquiries

### Expansion — 12 months

- 40–60 genuinely useful first-party/evidence pages
- dominant brand SERP and strong project-author associations
- 25–40 meaningful external mentions/referring domains
- repeatable case-study → social → PR → search distribution loop
- regular qualified professional inbound

## Definition of done

SEO is not "done" when metadata is changed. The operating system is complete only when:

1. code is deployed;
2. pages are crawlable and in sitemap;
3. IndexNow is sent;
4. Search Console/Bing/Yandex properties are verified where owner access permits;
5. baseline data is recorded;
6. distribution links to the official site;
7. performance and conversions are reviewed continuously.
