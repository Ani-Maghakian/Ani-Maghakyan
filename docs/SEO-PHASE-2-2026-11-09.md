# Service and project conversions

Service hero sections do not show the documented 48-project / 2,300-episode portfolio or provide a direct production-inquiry path. A discussion postprocessor also converts the old “Discuss a project” label into a comment link. Book retailer links appear as citations rather than purchase actions.

This patch updates HY / EN / RU service metadata, adds proof and relevant-work links, preserves explicitly marked production inquiries through the discussion pass, and adds a project-specific B2B callout. First-contact guidance asks for company, format, service, scope and timing; budget is optional. Unpublished ideas and scripts are still excluded from first contact. Only the business email already published on the official site can be enabled with CONTACT_EMAIL.

Taknuvra gets separate retailer actions for the 2021 and 2024 editions. The 2021 Online Armenian Store page has an Add to cart control when checked on 2026-11-09. Abril Books lists the 2024 edition as out of stock, so its action describes the edition rather than promising availability. Book ISBNs and source citations are unchanged. No prices, inventory or Offer markup are invented.

The Russian Elen’s Diary metadata answers the episode-count search using the existing author filmography: two seasons, 421 episodes (197 + 224). Cast queries need confirmed cast data before copy should promise a cast list.

Full application build, full regression suite and visual review remain pending the complete application dependencies and current production source.

The data-track attributes distinguish production inquiries from book-retailer clicks. They are analytics hooks, not evidence that GA4 is configured or a retailer click became a sale. Sales attribution requires retailer reporting or a supported campaign integration. Measure completed qualified inquiries separately from contact clicks.
