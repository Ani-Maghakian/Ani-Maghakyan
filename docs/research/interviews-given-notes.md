# Interviews and professional coverage: provenance

Research date: 2026-09-17. Data file: `interviews-given.json`.

The collection contains 15 verified source records: 11 interviews/program appearances and 4 pieces of professional coverage. It is a substantial discoverable selection, not a claim that every interview ever published has been found. Searches covered Armenian, Russian and English spellings of Ani Maghakyan’s name. Sources identify the screenwriter through her credited series, writing work or interview context; unrelated people with similar names were excluded.

The localized titles are short editorial descriptions, not necessarily the publisher’s exact headline. Summaries are original paraphrases. The `language` field identifies the source’s language; English and Russian card text does not imply that the full linked article has those translations. Publication dates are taken from the original publisher. No uploaded video has been represented as watched in full.

## Editorial exclusions

Personal gossip and private-life reporting were excluded from the professional portfolio. Cyclowiki was used for discovery only because its claims include insufficient sourcing and a literary attribution inconsistent with the original source. NEWS.am STYLE `/arm/print/101897/` was opened but concerns a generic Armflix guide rather than an interview with Ani and was excluded.

The linked publishers retain their original articles and videos. The data file contains short original summaries and links, not reproduced interviews or transcripts.
